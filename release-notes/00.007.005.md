## New
- Cheat search: find where a game keeps a number (lives, health, money) and make your own cheat from it. In the pause menu, Cheats → Search for a Cheat: search for the value the game shows, or without a value for things like a health bar. Then play, pause, and pick what happened to it (changed, went up, went down by the value...) until only a few addresses are left. In Results, keep one at the value you want as a cheat, or set it just once. Works on 8, 16 and 32-bit values; Undo takes back a step.
- Type in your own GameShark codes with the PS5's on-screen keyboard (Cheats → Type In Codes).
- Your own cheats come first in the Cheats list and are kept with the game. Square renames one, changes its codes or deletes it; on a cheat of the list, Square makes your own copy to fix. They are saved in `/data/mupen64plus/cheats/<game>.custom.txt`, where you can also add codes from a PC.
- Cheats is in the pause menu for every game now, also the ones the cheat list has nothing for.
- The touchpad goes back to the game from any page of the pause menu (Cheats, the cheat search, Controller Layout).
